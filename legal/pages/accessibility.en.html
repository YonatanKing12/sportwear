We want everyone to be able to shop on our site comfortably. The site is built to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA, in line with Israeli Standard 5568.

What we've done

  • You can browse the whole site with the keyboard alone, with a clear marker of where you are on the page.
  • The site works with screen readers: buttons, fields and images have descriptions.
  • Color contrast meets the requirements, and you can enlarge the text without breaking the layout.
  • Full support for Hebrew and Arabic right to left, and for English left to right.
  • The site respects your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • We tested the site's pages with an automated accessibility checker and by navigating with the keyboard.

What isn't perfect yet

  • The payment page (checkout) is Shopify's; we didn't build it ourselves.
  • If you come across an image without a description, or something that doesn't work as it should, let us know and we'll fix it.

Found a problem?

If something on the site isn't accessible to you, we'd like to know and fix it. Write to us on the Contact Us page and tell us which page the problem is on. We'll get back to you soon.
